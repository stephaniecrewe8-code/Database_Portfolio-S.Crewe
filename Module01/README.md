## Task What is IoT
IoT (Internet of Things) refers to the storage and management of data generated by a large network of connected physical devices, such as sensors, machines, and smart appliances, that continuously collect and transmit measurements over the internet.

Domains and included areas:

-Smart home: lighting, thermostats, security cameras, smart locks, appliances.
-Energy and utilities: smart meters, smart grids, renewable energy monitoring.


Commonly used (data) protocols:

-MQTT (Message Queuing Telemetry Transport): lightweight publish/subscribe via a broker, The most widely used for sensors and low-bandwidth or unreliable networks.
-CoAP (Constrained Application Protocol): lightweight REST-like protocol over UPD for constrained devices.

Typical devices (appliance or micro controller):

-Smart speakers (Alexa, Google Home), security cameras, doorbells.
-Raspberry Pi, BeagleBone: small single-board computers that run Linux, often used as gateways.

Challenges:

-Device limitations: Low power, memory, and processing capacity
-Connectivity and scalability: Unreliable or low-bandwidth networks, latency, and offline operation.
